# The Moonfire

## Overview

The moonfire is the silver light that has watched over [[Moonharbor]] since time out of mind. It is no ordinary fire: it burns from the moon's reflection on the water and must be guided night after night by a keeper through [[The Beacon Lens]].

## Details

- It gives no warmth and consumes no fuel, but feeds on the attention of the one who tends it.
- If it goes out three nights in a row, the hidden cave beneath [[The Black Cliffs]] opens.
- Its only counter is the [[Shadow Lantern]] — light and dark of the same old craft.
