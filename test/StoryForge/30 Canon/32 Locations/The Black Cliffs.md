# The Black Cliffs

## Overview

The jagged rocks at the west cape of [[Moonharbor]], notorious for shoals and wrecks. Without the light of the [[Moonharbor Lighthouse]], ships run aground here — which is exactly what [[Silvan Nightweaver]] counts on.

## Geography

Steep basalt needles, and among them a hidden cave reachable only at low tide and without the moonfire.

## Atmosphere

Wind, spray, the shriek of gulls. In darkness, the eeriest place on the coast.
