# Moonharbor Lighthouse

## Overview

The lighthouse stands at the very end of the mole and holds in its lantern room [[The Beacon Lens]], through which [[The Moonfire]] shines. Keeper [[Tobias Ferro]] and his apprentice [[Mara Stoneway]] live on the lower floors.

## Description

One hundred and twenty steps of worn granite spiral up to the lantern room. At the very top, behind salt-blind glass, the lens rests on a brass mount that turns night after night.

## Atmosphere

It smells of hot metal and lamp oil. When the lens is working, the whole tower hums softly along.
