# The Salty Gull

## Overview

The oldest tavern on the quay, run by [[Elke Stoneway]]. A gathering point for every fisher, every rumour, and every frozen returnee — [[Jonas Kell]] and [[Mara Stoneway]] among them.

## Description

Low beams, a counter of wreck timber, faded sea charts on the walls. In the corner a stove roars, always with a pot of fish soup on top.

## Atmosphere

Warm, loud, smoke-hazed. Here you learn everything — you only have to listen long enough.
