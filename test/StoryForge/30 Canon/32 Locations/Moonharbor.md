# Moonharbor

## Overview

Moonharbor is a small port town on the rough north coast, named for the silver light its [[Moonharbor Lighthouse]] casts over the water each night. Fishing boats, narrow lanes, and the eternal smell of salt and tar.

## Geography

A sheltered bay, bounded to the west by [[The Black Cliffs]]. Its heart is the mole with the lighthouse; on the quay stands [[The Salty Gull]].

## Atmosphere

Busy by day, still by night — as long as the moonfire burns. When it goes out, a darkness creeps into the lanes that does not feel like ordinary night.
