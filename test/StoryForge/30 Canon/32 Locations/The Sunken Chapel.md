# The Sunken Chapel

## Overview

A half-flooded chapel out on the tidal flats, reachable only at low water. Its `world` points to [[The Drowned Coast]] — a region note that does not yet exist in the vault, so the location surfaces as a flagged root in the Locations tree (AC-F4.3, unresolved parent).

## Description

Barnacle-crusted pews, a font brimming with seawater, and a bell whose rope has long since rotted away. When the tide turns, the whole nave breathes.

## Atmosphere

Cold, green light and the constant drip of water. A place that remembers being holy.
