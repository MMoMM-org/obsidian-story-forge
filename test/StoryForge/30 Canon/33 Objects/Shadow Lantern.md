# Shadow Lantern

## Overview

A blackened lantern that gives no light but swallows it. [[Silvan Nightweaver]] uses it to smother [[The Moonfire]] from afar and bring his boats unseen to [[The Black Cliffs]].

## Description

Iron and smoke-glass, and inside a flame of cold darkness. Where its glow falls, even the stars fade.

## Significance

The counterpart to [[The Beacon Lens]]. As long as the shadow lantern burns, the tower cannot win — and the other way around.
