# The Beacon Lens

## Overview

A hand-ground crystal lens at the heart of the [[Moonharbor Lighthouse]]. Through it [[The Moonfire]] shines and becomes the silver beam that guards the bay. Tended by [[Tobias Ferro]].

## Description

Fist-sized, shot through with countless facets, and within it a spark that never quite dies — as long as it is fed.

## Significance

Without the lens the tower is only a tower. It is the one tool that can master the moonfire — and Silvan's [[Shadow Lantern]] is its exact counterpart.
