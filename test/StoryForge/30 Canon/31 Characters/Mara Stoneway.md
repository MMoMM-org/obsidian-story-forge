# Mara Stoneway

> [!info] Facts live in the frontmatter above — Bases and the reference panel read them. Prose follows below.

## Overview

Mara Stoneway is an apprentice at the [[Moonharbor Lighthouse]], learning from the old keeper [[Tobias Ferro]] the craft of tending the harbour light. Raised by her aunt [[Elke Stoneway]] in the harbour tavern [[The Salty Gull]], she knows every jetty and every gull in Moonharbor. Curious, stubborn, and quick to catch fire — hence her nickname "Spark".

## Appearance

Short copper-red hair that never holds still in the wind, and a face full of freckles. On the back of her left hand she carries a burn scar — a keepsake from the night she first lit the hot beacon lens on her own.

## Personality

Inquisitive to the point of stubbornness. Where others look away, Mara climbs after. She has little patience for excuses and even less for people who harm the harbour.

## Goals & Motivation

She wants to prove she deserves the old keeper's legacy — and to find out why the moonfire is going dark.

## Relationships

- **[[Tobias Ferro]]** — master and mentor; the only one who truly teaches her anything.
- **[[Jonas Kell]]** — fisher, best friend, and more in the making.
- **[[Elke Stoneway]]** — aunt and foster mother.
- **[[Silvan Nightweaver]]** — the shadow she hunts.
