# Elke Stoneway

> [!info] Facts live in the frontmatter above — Bases and the reference panel read them. Prose follows below.

## Overview

Elke Stoneway runs [[The Salty Gull]], the oldest tavern on the harbour, and raised her niece [[Mara Stoneway]] after the girl's parents were lost at sea. Whatever is whispered in Moonharbor, Elke hears it first — at her counter everyone talks themselves into trouble.

## Personality

Warm, resolute, with an ear for every rumour. Anyone who threatens her niece takes on the whole taproom.
