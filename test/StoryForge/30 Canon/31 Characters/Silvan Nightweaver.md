# Silvan Nightweaver

> [!info] Facts live in the frontmatter above — Bases and the reference panel read them. Prose follows below.

## Overview

Silvan Nightweaver lives off the dark. With his [[Shadow Lantern]] he smothers the harbour light so his boats can land unseen at [[The Black Cliffs]]. To [[Tobias Ferro]] he is an old enemy; to [[Mara Stoneway]] he becomes the first person she truly fears.

## Personality

Polite, quiet, patient — and utterly without conscience. He never threatens; he lets the darkness speak for him.

## Goals & Motivation

As long as [[The Moonfire]] burns, his trade is dead. So it must go out — for good.
