# Jonas Kell

> [!info] Facts live in the frontmatter above — Bases and the reference panel read them. Prose follows below.

## Overview

Jonas Kell has been going to sea since he was twelve. He knows the currents around [[The Black Cliffs]] and is the first to notice strange boats sliding into the harbour at night without lights. [[Mara Stoneway]] trusts him blindly — and he trusts her.

## Personality

Calm, dependable, braver than he admits. Where Mara flares up, Jonas holds the course.

## Relationships

- **[[Mara Stoneway]]** — friend, and perhaps soon more.
- **[[Elke Stoneway]]** — gets his best catch for the kitchen of [[The Salty Gull]].
