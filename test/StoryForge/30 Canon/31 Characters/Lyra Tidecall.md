# Lyra Tidecall

> [!info] Facts live in the frontmatter above — Bases and the reference panel read them. Prose follows below.

## Overview

Lyra Tidecall is a Merfolk tide-speaker who has haunted the waters off [[Moonharbor]] for longer than the lighthouse has stood. She comes ashore only when the moonfire is in danger — the light that feeds the tides she speaks to. To most harbour folk she is a rumour, a shimmer seen between waves; to [[Mara Stoneway]] she becomes an unlikely ally.

She and [[Silvan Nightweaver]] share a history: he once tried to bind her tide-gift to the [[Shadow Lantern]], and she refused at great cost. That refusal is the reason she now watches the harbour light with such intent.

Bioluminescent scale markings trace her collarbone and forearms — blue-white in the dark, invisible in daylight. Ashore she wears long sleeves and tide-bleached coats. Her webbed fingers give her away only on close inspection.

## Personality

Patient with tides. Impatient with hesitation. She speaks rarely and entirely in metaphor when pressed, which people find either illuminating or maddening. She respects Mara immediately because the girl climbs toward danger rather than away from it.

## Goals & Motivation

Protect [[The Moonfire]] from extinction — not out of sentiment, but because three nights of darkness opens the cave beneath [[The Black Cliffs]], and what wakes there is older than the moonfire itself.

## Relationships

- **[[Mara Stoneway]]** — the keeper's spark; Lyra sees her own younger stubbornness reflected back.
- **[[Silvan Nightweaver]]** — old enemy; his shadow-craft nearly unmade her gift once.
