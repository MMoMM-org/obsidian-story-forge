# The Salty Gull

<!-- Prose below. Facts belong in Canon, not here. Link entities with [[wikilinks]]
     so the reference panel and /continuity-check can resolve them. -->

Inside [[The Salty Gull]] it was warm and loud, and that was exactly what Mara needed now. Her aunt [[Elke Stoneway]] set a bowl of fish soup in front of her without a word and studied her across the counter. "You look like the tower — cold and without light."

[[Jonas Kell]] pulled his stool closer. He had been out that evening. "Two boats," he said quietly, "no running lights, heading for the west cape. In this dark they'll stave in their bows on [[The Black Cliffs]] — unless someone knows the shoals blind."

Elke wiped a glass dry, slowing. "There's talk," she said. "A stranger who only comes by night. Gloves, even in summer. They call him the shadow trader." She looked at Mara. "And your master went white as chalk the last time I said the name aloud."
