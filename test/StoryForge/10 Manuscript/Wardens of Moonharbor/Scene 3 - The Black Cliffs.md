# The Black Cliffs

<!-- Prose below. Facts belong in Canon, not here. Link entities with [[wikilinks]]
     so the reference panel and /continuity-check can resolve them. -->

At low tide the west cape gave up its path. Mara climbed down among the basalt needles of [[The Black Cliffs]], [[Tobias Ferro]] close behind her, his cane in one hand and a storm lantern in the other.

In the hidden cave a cold darkness burned. [[Silvan Nightweaver]] stood bent over the [[Shadow Lantern]], and where its glow fell, even the stars faded in the cave mouth. He did not so much as turn. "The old keeper," he whispered. "And his spark. You've come to fetch your moonfire back." A thin smile. "But light and shadow are the same craft, child. You need only know which lantern to put out first."

Mara thought of the cold [[The Beacon Lens]] high up in the tower — and that it was only the second night. She had one left.
