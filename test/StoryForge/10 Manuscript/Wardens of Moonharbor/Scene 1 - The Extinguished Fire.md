# The Extinguished Fire

<!-- Prose below. Facts belong in Canon, not here. Link entities with [[wikilinks]]
     so the reference panel and /continuity-check can resolve them. -->

Mara knew the one hundred and twenty steps in her sleep, yet tonight she counted every one. Something was wrong. No hum lay in the walls of the [[Moonharbor Lighthouse]], no warm smell of brass — only a cold that seeped down from above.

Up in the lantern room, [[The Beacon Lens]] stood still on its mount. The spark within it, the one that never quite went out, had shrivelled to a grey nothing. Outside, the bay lay black, as if someone had blown out the moon itself.

"Don't touch it." [[Tobias Ferro]] came up the stairs, slower than usual, his cane hard on the stone. His face in the starlight was grey as the lens. "That didn't go out, girl. That was put out." He looked out over the water, to where [[The Moonfire]] should have been dancing. "And I know only one man who can do that."
