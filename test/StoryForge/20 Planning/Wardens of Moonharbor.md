# Wardens of Moonharbor

A small test story in three scenes. The harbour light of [[Moonharbor]] goes out,
and the lighthouse apprentice [[Mara Stoneway]] follows the cause — all the way to
[[Silvan Nightweaver]] and his [[Shadow Lantern]].
