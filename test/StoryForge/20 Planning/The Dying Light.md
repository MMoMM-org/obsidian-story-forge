# The Dying Light

The thread around the fading [[The Moonfire]]: who puts the light out, why, and
what happens if it stays dark three nights in a row. Carries [[Mara Stoneway]]
from the [[Moonharbor Lighthouse]] through [[The Salty Gull]] to [[The Black Cliffs]].
